# Brand & Visual Guidelines

*Extracted from the Cribs Management landing page screenshot. All hex values and measurements are visual estimates from a flattened image, so confirm them with an eyedropper or browser DevTools before locking them in.*

## 1. Brand Personality

- **Feel:** modern, minimal, premium SaaS with a calm, trustworthy fintech tone.
- **Look:** high-contrast black and white with one muted green accent, generous whitespace, soft rounded shapes.
- **Voice:** short, confident headlines ending in full stops ("Modular Control. Infinite Scale."), "say goodbye to…" benefit framing, plain language, and local details (KSh, M-Pesa, Paystack) that signal a Kenyan market.

### Status and icon tints (pale tile plus matching darker glyph or text)

| Tint | Background | Foreground | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Colour rules**

- Sections alternate between charcoal and light, which creates the page rhythm.
- White cards sit on charcoal for maximum contrast.
- Green is used sparingly, only for "success", "verified" and "recommended".
- Black is the primary action colour, so the green accent never competes with the main CTA.

## 3. Typography

- **Typeface:** a clean neo-grotesque or geometric sans, closest to **Inter** (Geist or Satoshi also fit). Use one family throughout.
- **Hero headline:** very large (about 64–80px desktop), semi-bold, tight tracking, line-height about 1.05. The second line is set in *italic* for emphasis, and the whole headline is wrapped in parentheses: *( Run your real estate on autopilot. )*
- **Section headings (H2):** 36–44px, weight 600, tight letter-spacing, centred in card-grid sections and left-aligned in split sections.
- **Card titles (H3):** 16–18px, weight 600.
- **Body:** 14–16px, weight 400, line-height about 1.6, muted grey.
- **Micro labels:** 10–12px, **UPPERCASE**, wide letter-spacing, weight 500–600. Used for "EXPLORE CAPABILITY ›", footer column headings and badges.
- **Prices:** large (28–32px) medium weight, with the "/month" suffix small and muted.

## 4. Layout and Spacing

- **Container:** max width about 1200px, side padding about 24–32px.
- **Section padding:** generous, about 80–100px top and bottom.
- **Grids:** 3 columns for feature cards (2 rows), 4 columns for pricing, 2 columns (visual plus text) for feature deep-dives. The visual and text sides alternate left and right.
- **Gaps:** about 16–24px between cards.
- **Section order:** Hero, Modular feature grid (dark), Split feature (light), Split feature (dark), Split feature (light), Pricing (dark), CTA banner, Footer (dark).

## 5. Shape, Elevation and Borders

- **Radius:** buttons are fully rounded pills (999px), cards 16–20px, large mockup containers 24px, small badges pill-shaped, inner mini-cards 12–16px.
- **Elevation:** nearly flat. Cards rely on contrast rather than shadows, with at most a very soft shadow.
- **Borders:** 1px hairlines (light grey on light backgrounds, translucent white on dark). The featured pricing card gets a 2px green border.

## 6. Iconography

- Outline style with a thin, consistent stroke (about 1.5px), 20–24px, Lucide or Feather style.
- Icons sit top-left in feature cards, or inside a small rounded pastel tile in mockups.
- Checklists use a circle-check outline icon at about 14–16px.
- Link affordance is a small chevron (›) or arrow (→).

## 7. Components

### Navigation bar

- Transparent over the hero and sticky.
- Logo on the left, centred links in small text (Features, Properties, Payments, Automations, Roles, Security, Pricing).
- On the right: theme toggle (moon icon), "Sign In" as a plain text link, and "Get Started" as a black pill with white text.

### Buttons

- **Primary:** black pill, white text, 14px medium, optional trailing arrow ("Start Managing Free →").
- **Secondary (on photo):** translucent or outlined pill with a white border ("View Live Demo").
- **Card button (pricing):** full-width pill with a light grey outline and dark text.
- **Featured button:** solid sage green pill with white text and an arrow.
- **CTA banner button:** black pill.

### Feature cards (dark section)

- White card on charcoal with a 16–20px radius and about 24px padding.
- Stack, top to bottom: outline icon, bold title, 2–3 lines of muted body text, uppercase "EXPLORE CAPABILITY ›" link.

### Split feature sections

- **Text side:** line-art icon, H2, a paragraph, then a 3-item checklist with circle-check icons.
- **Visual side:** a large rounded container with a faint border containing 2 stacked, slightly offset white mini-cards. Each mini-card has a pastel icon tile, a title, a subtitle and an optional status pill ("ACTIVE", amber).
- **Payment variant (on dark):** a dark card with a thin light border showing a "VERIFIED" mint pill, a small label ("Amount Paid"), a large amount (**KSh 45,000**), a divider, then label and value rows (Method, Receipt No.).

### Pricing cards

- Four equal white cards: Pay-as-you-go (Free), Basic (KES 3,500), Pro (KES 6,500) and Enterprise (KES 10,500).
- Content order: plan name, one-line descriptor in grey, price with "/month", divider, feature list with check icons, muted "+N more features" line, then a bottom-aligned button.
- **Featured plan:** green 2px border plus a "Most Popular" dark-green badge pinned to the top edge, and a solid green button.

### CTA banner

- A wide soft-grey rounded panel centred on the dark background, with a bold headline ("Ready to elevate your standards?"), one supporting line and a black pill button.

### Footer

- Charcoal background. Logo and tagline on the left, then three link columns with uppercase micro headings (Platform Core, Company, Legal & Privacy).
- Disabled or coming-soon links are dimmer grey. The bottom row holds the copyright and credits in very small muted text.

## 8. Imagery

- **Hero:** a full-bleed aerial drone photograph of a suburban estate (green lawns, terracotta roofs, pools) under a dark gradient overlay (about 40–50% black) so white text stays legible.
- **Product visuals:** UI-style mockup cards rather than stock photos or illustrations.
- No gradients or decorative blobs elsewhere, so the colour comes from the photo and the pastel tiles.

## 9. Light and Dark Behaviour

- The page uses a dark and light alternating pattern and includes a theme toggle in the nav.
- Dark mode reverses to charcoal backgrounds with white cards, and muted text moves to #A3A3A3.

## 11. Do and Don't

**Do**

- Keep one accent colour and let black carry the main actions.
- Use whitespace and large type for hierarchy instead of heavy decoration.
- Keep icons in a single outline style and one stroke weight.
- Localise details (currency, payment methods) to the target market.

**Don't**

- Add drop shadows, gradients or extra accent colours.
- Mix icon styles or typefaces.
- Use green for ordinary buttons, since it signals success or "recommended".
- Copy the layout or the brand elements one-to-one. Use this as a reference for the *system*, and swap in your own logo, colour accent and imagery so your project keeps its own identity.

## 13. Reusable Section Header

Every section from 3 onward opens the same way:

1. **Eyebrow:** a small uppercase label with a dot or icon, above the heading.
2. **H2 in two parts:** a plain statement in the bold sans, then the benefit phrase in the italic serif accent, usually on its own line.
3. **Sub-paragraph:** one or two lines, centred, narrow (about 640px max).

Build this once as a component and reuse it for every section.

## 14. Navbar

- Logo on the left, linking to #home.
- Centre: anchor links that scroll to section ids (proposed labels: Features, Platform, Marketplace, How it works, Mobile, FAQ).
- Right: a text link for the marketplace plus a primary pill button for the management portal (proposed: Browse listings and Open management, matching the hero).
- Sticky on scroll. On mobile it collapses to a hamburger and the two CTAs move into the drawer.
- Anchor targets need scroll-margin-top equal to the navbar height.

## 15. Hero Configuration (#home)

### Element order

1. **H1, three lines, centred (max about 900px):** line 1 upright bold, *line 2 in the italic serif accent*, line 3 upright bold.
   - Line 1: Run your properties.
   - Line 2: Fill your vacancies.
   - Line 3: All in one simple place.
2. **Supporting paragraph:** 1–2 lines, centred, covers both audiences (landlords and renters).
3. **Audience switch (inferred):** two small tab-style controls under the paragraph, one for landlords and agents and one for renters.
4. **CTA pair:** two buttons side by side.
   - Primary: **Browse listings**, linking to the marketplace listings page.
   - Secondary: **Open management**, linking to the management portal (sign-in or dashboard).
5. **Hero visual:** a wide browser-frame mockup, centred below the CTAs. Inside: a marketplace search bar, a listing card (building photo, title, location, bedrooms, price, action button) and one or two small floating status cards.
6. **Stat strip:** four counters in one row directly under the mockup (big number, short label). The figures in the screenshot are not legible, so treat the numbers as placeholders.

## 16. Section Patterns

- **Bento grid (sections 4 and 5):** 3 columns on desktop with mixed 1×1 and 2×1 tiles. Each tile has a small label, a big stat or title, one line of description and a mini UI visual. Stats such as 2.5×, 40%, under 1 min, 100% and 2-in-1 are used as tile headlines.
- **Hub-and-spoke (section 6):** a central logo node, with inputs listed on the left and outcomes (a short checklist) on the right, linked by lines.
- **Stepper (section 7):** 4 numbered steps in a row, each with an icon, a short title and one line of text. It stacks vertically on mobile.
- **Two-product split (section 8):** two equal cards, each with an eyebrow, a title, a feature checklist and a button. One card leads to management, the other to the marketplace.
- **4×2 feature grid (sections 9 and 10):** icon, title and one-line description per cell, followed by a product preview below the grid (rent roll table in 9, search results in 10).
- **Phone with callouts (section 11):** one centred phone mockup with small floating cards on either side.
- **Form plus steps (section 12):** left is a sign-up card with an email field and button; right is a 3-row list where the first row is expanded and highlighted.
- **Accordion (section 13):** one column, hairline dividers, a chevron per row, one row open at a time.
- **CTA band (section 14):** centred two-part headline and two buttons (primary and secondary).

## 17. Footer Structure

- **Top row, left:** the line "Two products. One account.", a short description, product buttons and a row of social icons.
- **Top row, right:** 3–4 link columns (proposed: Product, Company, Resources, Legal).
- **Bottom:** a full-width oversized CRIBSKENYA wordmark cropped by the page edge, with a small copyright strip below it.

## 18. Fonts

The type system has **two voices**:

1. **Sans (headlines, UI, body):** a clean geometric or grotesque sans, semi-bold to bold for headings with tight tracking. Closest matches: Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Manrope, DM Sans.
2. **Italic serif (accent):** used for the second half of every H2 and for line 2 of the hero. High-contrast, elegant italic. Closest matches: Instrument Serif Italic, Playfair Display Italic, Newsreader Italic, Fraunces Italic.
3. **Wordmark:** the oversized footer logotype is heavy and condensed, and CRIBS and KENYA seem to use two different styles. Verify against the real logo file.

**Confirm before you commit:** the exact families can't be identified from a screenshot this small. Open the live page, then DevTools, Elements, Computed, and read Rendered Fonts. You can also check the Google Fonts link in the page head, or use the WhatFont extension.

**If you can't confirm,** use Plus Jakarta Sans plus Instrument Serif (both free on Google Fonts).

## 19. Headline Copy Pattern

Every H2 follows the same formula, so reuse it for new sections:

**\[Plain statement.\]** *\[Italic benefit phrase.\]*

Examples from the page: "Property management, *the way it was always meant to be*" and "Frequently asked questions, *answered simply*". Keep the plain part to a short noun phrase or question, and make the italic part the outcome the user cares about.
